Why we built our own brand.

Most contract manufacturers have never held a licence in their own name. They build to someone else's file. When a regulator asks a hard question, the customer has to answer it.

We did it the other way round. VIREXA is registered on our manufacturing licence for all twenty-six devices. We wrote the device master record. We passed the inspection. We carry the responsibility.

That is the argument we make to an OEM. We have already done under our own name what you would ask us to do under yours.
